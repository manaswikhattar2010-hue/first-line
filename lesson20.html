The Missing Map

Diamond Ridge · the path of fire

You reach for the map before setting out and find your bag empty instead — no map, no explanation, just a single scorched scrap of parchment caught on a bramble nearby. The handwriting on it is smudged nearly past reading.

Every string carries its own tools

A string isn't just letters sitting still — it comes with methods built in, ready to use with a dot: .strip() to trim off stray space, .lower() or .upper() to even out the case, .replace() to swap one thing for another.

From the spellbook:
scrap = " NiCoLe " print(scrap.strip().lower())
nicole
.strip().lower().upper().replace().split()

Try it yourself. Clean up the scrap however you like — the house only cares that you use at least one string method on it, and speak the result aloud with print().

Once the smudges fall away, a name resolves clearly: Nicole. Beneath it, barely legible, three more words — outskirts of Sol-Torren. Whoever took your map, it seems, wants you to find her first.
