CHAPTER XII by candlelight

Four Doorsteps

You've arrived here without a path to follow. Choose the gem you carried, and the house will remember where it leads.

Ruby — Autumn's Grove Sapphire — Virelia's Palace Emerald — Aspen's Castle Amethyst — Buer's Palace

Virelia's Palace

the path of water

Nadir Haven opens onto a palace grown rather than built — coral and pearl fused into archways, currents combed neatly through open halls. "Come inside, if you'd like," Virelia says, gesturing loosely toward the doors. "Or wait in the gardens. Either way, you're welcome here."

A spell with no name

Not every current needs a name to be useful. Some are cast once, used immediately, and let go without ever being written down properly. In the spellbook's tongue, this is called a lambda — a function with no name, written on a single line, used exactly where it's needed and never spoken of again.

From the spellbook:
add = lambda a, b: a + b print(add(2, 3))
5

It works the same as a spell written with def — it just skips the ceremony of naming and indenting a whole block beneath it. That makes it most useful when you need a tiny spell just once, often handed directly to another spell that's expecting one.

From the spellbook:
def apply_twice(f, x): return f(f(x)) result = apply_twice(lambda n: n + 3, 1) print(result)
7

Here, the lambda was never given a name at all — it was simply written, used, and forgotten, the moment it was handed to apply_twice.

at the doors

Virelia waits by the threshold, patient, unbothered either way.

Step Inside

Follow her through the coral archways and into the palace proper.
Wait in the Gardens

Stay outside among the drifting light, and see what waits there instead.

the path of fire

"You can come sit with us," Autumn says, nodding toward the warmth beyond the tree line, "or stay at the edge if crowds aren't your thing. No pressure either way."
