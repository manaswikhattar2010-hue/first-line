Phlograna

the Lava Court · land of the Vulcans

The border crossing alone nearly takes your breath away — rivers of molten stone winding between black glass spires, heat that should be unbearable and somehow isn't. A woman with skin like cooling embers meets you before you've even finished staring, and her face breaks into a grin the moment she spots Ember beside you.

"Ember! It's been ages." She barely glances at Autumn before pulling Ember into a hug. "And who's this you've brought me?"

Autumn clears her throat, faintly put out at being skipped over, but Lilith's attention is already on you, warm and curious rather than suspicious.

"Any friend of Ember's eats at my table," Lilith says. "Come in, come in — mind the floor near the east wall, it's still a bit temperamental."

at Lilith's table

Lilith listens to why you've come with genuine interest, nodding along, occasionally glancing at Ember for confirmation rather than Autumn. Whatever's between her and Autumn, it clearly isn't unfriendliness — just distance. You get the sense this isn't the last you'll hear of it.

Sneznova

the Ice Court · land of the Frost Elves

You don't even see it coming — one moment you're crossing into a field of blue-white frost, and the next, ice has closed around both your wrists and Virelia's, hard and absolute. A tall figure steps out from between the frozen trees, unhurried, studying you both like specimens.

"Trespassers," the frost elf says flatly. "In Sneznova, that's rarely a mistake anyone makes twice."

Then her eyes land on Virelia properly, and something in her posture shifts entirely.

"...A siren?" She steps closer, ice already loosening around Virelia's wrists. "Why didn't you say so. Our courts have kept peace for longer than I've been alive — you should have led with that."

Sylvia's apology

The ice at your own wrists melts a moment later, almost as an afterthought. "Sylvia," the frost elf offers, by way of introduction. "Sorry about that. Standard procedure, but I should've looked more carefully before I froze first and asked questions second."

Krystaline

the Light Court · land of the Valkyrie

The light here doesn't come from any sun you recognize — it simply exists, even, shadowless, faintly uncomfortable in a way you can't quite name. A woman in pale armor blocks the path before you've gone more than a few steps into it, wings half-spread, expression carved from something colder than the metal she's wearing.

"State your purpose," she says, "and choose your next words carefully. I don't extend patience twice."

Aspen answers for you, calm as ever, but Celeste's eyes stay fixed on you the entire time he speaks — like she's measuring something in you that has nothing to do with anything he's actually saying.

Hawthorne

the Nightmare Court · land of the Ghouls

Hawthorne doesn't announce itself so much as arrive around you — one moment there's a road, and the next there's a court built from something that looks like bone and remembers being alive. A gaunt figure unfolds itself from the shadows near the gate, too many joints bending in ways that make you look away on instinct.

"Buer." The ghoul's voice is dry, delighted. "You brought me a visitor. How thoughtful."

"Crave," Buer says, with none of his usual charm. "I need your help. You know the terms."

Crave's attention slides toward you, slow and deliberate, before returning to Buer entirely.

"I do. An offering, Buer. A human body, for my trouble. You know I don't work for free — you brought exactly the sort of thing I like best, didn't you."

Buer doesn't answer right away. You don't like how long the silence lasts.
