CHAPTER XVII by candlelight

Four Allies

You've arrived here without a path to follow. Choose who you're with, and the house will remember.

Ruby — with Autumn Sapphire — with Virelia Emerald — with Aspen Amethyst — with Buer

Asking Autumn

the path of fire

Ember walks you back through the grove, and by the time you reach the tree line, Autumn is already waiting, arms crossed, watching the two of you approach with open curiosity of her own.

"So. What exactly do you need from me?"

Rather than explain it all at once, you find it easier to simply list it out — and a list, once made, doesn't have to stay the way you first wrote it. You can add to it with .append(), take something off with .remove(), or put it in order with .sort().

From the spellbook:
needs = ["a way home", "answers"] needs.append("Autumn's help") print(needs)
['a way home', 'answers', "Autumn's help"]

A few other list methods worth knowing:

.append(x).remove(x).sort().pop()len(list)

Try it yourself. Build a list of what you need, then use at least one list method on it — add something, take something away, sort it, whatever fits what you're asking Autumn for.

Autumn's answer

Autumn's whole face lights up before you've even finished. "Obviously I'm helping. Are you kidding? Let's go."

She hesitates for exactly one second before adding:

Phlograna — the Lava Court

Land of the Vulcans. "You're going to need them for this," Autumn says. "I'll take you as far as the border myself."
