The attack comes from the tree line without warning — a figure in dark leathers, blade already drawn, faster than you have time to properly react to. Autumn moves before you do, and when you glance at her face, it isn't anger you find there. It's something closer to grief.

The assassin doesn't flinch from the fire building in front of her — if anything, she looks almost resigned to it. "Evelyn," she offers, like a name might buy her a moment she clearly doesn't expect to get.
