CHAPTER III by candlelight

The Fork in the Path

The corridor splits ahead of you: one way glows, the other stays dark, and which one lights depends on a question the house silently asks of anyone who passes.

This is called a condition — a fork that is only taken when something is true. Here, the house asks whether a traveler's age is 18 or older.

From the spellbook:
if age >= 18:
    print("You may pass")
You may pass

Notice two things: the first line ends in a colon, and the line underneath is indented. That indentation tells the house this only happens when the condition above is true.

Try it yourself. Write an if that checks whether age is 18 or older, and have it speak a message underneath, indented, the same way it's shown above. There's more than one way to phrase the condition — the exact wording of your message doesn't matter, so long as the shape is right.
