CHAPTER VIII by candlelight

The Gem Table

A low table stands in the middle of the room, scattered with gems of every colour, each catching the candlelight differently. Beside them, a blank ledger waits — and a note, in handwriting older than yours.

"Write down what is on the table," it says, "and something will be added to it that was not there before."

In the spellbook's tongue, a row of things held under one name is called a list. It looks like this — a name, an equals sign, and square brackets holding whatever you like, each one separated by a comma.

From the spellbook:
gems = ["ruby", "sapphire", "emerald"]
print(gems)
['ruby', 'sapphire', 'emerald']

A list can hold as few or as many things as you like — one gem, or a dozen. If you remember the Spiral Stair, you can even walk through your list one item at a time:

for gem in gems: print(gem)

Try it yourself. Write down what's on the table — real gems, invented ones, or something else entirely. The ledger only asks that it takes the shape of a list: a name, an equals sign, and square brackets holding at least one thing. What you put inside, how many, and whether you loop through them afterward — all of that is yours to decide.
