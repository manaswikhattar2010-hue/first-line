Nicole's House

the path of fire

The cottage sits at the very edge of Sol-Torren, where the heat gives way to something almost temperate. Evelyn walks right up to the door like she's done it a hundred times. Autumn hangs back half a step, watching the place with open suspicion she doesn't bother hiding.

The door opens before Evelyn even knocks. "You brought a guest," Nicole says, delighted rather than alarmed, waving you inside without a shred of the wariness Autumn's clearly holding onto for her. "Come in, come in — let's see about that map of yours."

You're barely two steps inside when the floor gives way beneath something unseen — and iron, glowing hot, snaps closed around Aspen's wrists and ankles before he can so much as react. He doesn't scream, but his jaw goes tight enough that you can see it.
