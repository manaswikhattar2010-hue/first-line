Autumn's Wildfire

the path of fire

The ruby's warmth spreads up your arm as the path opens onto a smoke-thick clearing. A woman crouches at its center, fists clenched, fire licking up her arms and catching in the dry brush around her. Trees are already blackening at the edges.

"I can't stop it," Autumn says through gritted teeth, her shifted form fighting her, flame spilling out faster than she can pull it back. "Whatever you're going to do — do it now."

Aspen's Question

the path of earth

The emerald settles heavy in your hand as root-tangled dark closes in around the path. A figure steps out from between two trees, blocking your way entirely. Antlers catch what little light there is. His eyes don't blink.

"Trespasser," he says, not unkindly, but not warmly either. "These are Fae lands, and you were not invited. Explain yourself, and explain it properly — or leave the way you came. I am Aspen, and I am not easily convinced."

Aspen isn't interested in threats or pleading — only in whether you can shape a proper answer: something named with def, and then actually called, so your words are truly spoken rather than merely intended.

One way it might look:
