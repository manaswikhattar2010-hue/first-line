CHAPTER XIX by candlelight

Four Confessions

You've arrived here without a path to follow. Choose the court you're in, and the house will remember.

Ruby — with Lilith Sapphire — with Sylvia Emerald — with Celeste Amethyst — with Crave

The Way Home

with Lilith

Lilith watches you for a while before she finally asks it — not unkindly, just directly, the way she seems to do everything.

"So. Why have you actually come here?"

Turning words into numbers

Lilith counts something out on her fingers before she answers properly — and when the house counts, it needs a true number to work with, not just the shape of one. This is type conversion: turning a value from one kind into another, like int() to make something a number, or str() to make it words again.

From the spellbook:
pearls_needed = "4" pearls_needed = int(pearls_needed) print(pearls_needed + 1)
5

Written as "4", it's just text — the house can't add anything to it. Converted with int(), it becomes an actual number, one that can be counted, compared, added to.

"What you need," Lilith says, "isn't something I can just hand you. There's an old working bound to you — a tether keeping you here. Break it, and you go home. To break it, you'll need four broach pearls. Find all four, and the spell unravels."

where to look

Lilith thinks for a moment, then offers you two places to start.

Diamond Ridge

"Cold, high up, and picked over by half the realm already. But pearls do turn up there."
Krahn Forest
