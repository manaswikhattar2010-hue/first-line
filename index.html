CHAPTER I by candlelight

The Spoken Word

The corridor ends at a heavy oak door. No handle, no keyhole — only words carved faintly into the wood, worn smooth by travelers who came before you. A single candle gutters on the sill beside an old, dog-eared spellbook.

You open the spellbook to its first page. It explains that every door in this house answers to an incantation, and the simplest of them all is print(). Whatever words you place inside the parentheses — held safely inside quotation marks — are the words that will appear, glowing, on the door before you.

From the spellbook:
print("Hello World")
Hello World

Speak the incantation exactly as it is written above, and the door will open.
