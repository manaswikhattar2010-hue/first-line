CHAPTER IX by candlelight

The Spellsmith's Bench

Past the gem table, a narrow stair leads down into a workshop thick with the smell of hot metal and old paper. An old spellsmith looks up from the bench, wipes their hands, and waves you closer.

"Every spell you've cast so far," they say, "you've cast once, by hand, right where you stood. Let me show you how to forge one you can carry with you — something you name once, and call upon as many times as you like."

Naming a spell

In the old tongue, this is called a function. You write def, followed by a name of your choosing and a pair of parentheses, then indent the instructions beneath it — same as the fork and the stair. Writing it doesn't cast it yet. You have to call its name afterward for it to do anything.

From the spellbook:
def greet_traveler(): print("Welcome, traveler.")
greet_traveler()
Welcome, traveler.

Handing it something

A spell can also ask for something when you call it — a name placed inside the parentheses, which the spell then knows how to use. This is called a parameter.

def greet(name): print("Welcome, " + name) greet("Elior") Welcome, Elior

Getting something back

Some spells don't speak at all — they simply hand something back to you, quietly, using return. You can then catch what they hand back in a variable, the same way you've caught a value before.

def double(n): return n * 2 result = double(4) print(result) 8

That's the whole shape of it: a name, a set of instructions beneath it, and a call whenever you'd like it to run. Whether it speaks, hands something back, or both — that part is always up to you.

your reward

The spellsmith reaches beneath the bench and sets four gems in front of you, each humming faintly with something different. "Take one," they say. "It will decide which way the house opens for you next."

leads to fire
Ruby

A gem that burns warm in your palm, restless and quick to answer.

 leads to water
Sapphire

A gem cool to the touch, patient, and slow to give up its secrets.

 leads to earth
Emerald

A gem heavy and steady, the kind that doesn't move once it's set.

 leads to shadow
Amethyst

A gem that seems darker the longer you look at it.
