CHAPTER V by candlelight

The Wishing Well

Past the Archive, the path ends at an old stone well, its water black and still except for a scatter of coins glinting far below. There is no door here, no fork, nothing to get right or wrong — only the water, waiting to hear what you have to say.

Speak whatever you like into the well: something from the rooms you've already passed through, or words of your own the house has never heard. Unlike the door and the fork, the well isn't listening for anything in particular. It simply shows you whatever your words bring to life.

A wish someone left behind:
wish = "a library that never closes"
