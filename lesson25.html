The Celestial Isle

the path of fire

The road simply ends. No warning, no fork — the path drops away into a chasm so deep you can't see the bottom of it, mist curling up from somewhere far below. Evelyn stops short, swearing under her breath.

"That's new," Evelyn says. "This wasn't on the map an hour ago."

The same working as before

You've woven words into values before — an f-string, slotting whatever you need directly into the middle of a sentence with curly braces. Right now, you need something solid enough to walk on.

From the spellbook:
length = 40 print(f"A bridge of light stretches {length} feet across the void.")
A bridge of light stretches 40 feet across the void.

Try it yourself. Weave a value into a bridge of your own making, and speak it into being with print().

Light knits itself into something solid underfoot, and you cross without looking down. On the other side, though, there's no road waiting — just open sky, and the ground you're standing on drifting slowly, untethered, high above everything. An island. Floating. Nowhere near where you should be.

Evelyn goes very still, piecing something together. "The map," she says slowly. "She hexed the map. This whole time, we've been reading exactly what she wanted us to read."

The Dragon Cliff

the path of earth

You step out of Nicole's house expecting her garden, and instead find open air and stone underfoot — a wide cliff edge, wind cutting sideways, and scattered across it like boulders that happen to breathe, four enormous dragons, asleep.

"That," Celeste whispers, barely moving her lips, "is not where we were a second ago."

The same working as before

You've woven words into values before — an f-string, slotting whatever you need directly into the middle of a sentence with curly braces. Right now, you need something quiet enough not to wake anything up.

From the spellbook:
steps = "silent" print(f"Each footstep falls {steps} against the stone.")
Each footstep falls silent against the stone.

Try it yourself. Weave a value into a working of your own, and speak it quietly into being with print().

You make it nearly halfway across the ledge, Celeste a half step ahead, before the nearest dragon's eye slides open — slow, enormous, and entirely focused on the two of you.

The Camp

the path of fire

You find the camp exactly where the numbers pointed — a rough circle of tents, a dozen guards, and at the center, chained to a post, Autumn, soot-streaked but unmistakably alive. Lilith grips your arm. "We need a distraction. A big one."

The same working as before

You've woven words into values before — an f-string, slotting whatever you need directly into the middle of a sentence with curly braces. Right now, you need something loud enough to turn every head in the camp.

From the spellbook:
color = "crimson" print(f"The sky bursts into {color} light!")
The sky bursts into crimson light!

Try it yourself. Weave a value into a firework of your own, and set it off with print().

The sky splits open with color, and every guard in the camp turns to look at once. You and Lilith move fast, reaching Autumn in seconds — but the chains holding her are woven through with a working neither of you recognizes, and nothing you try finds the point where it actually gives. Before you can try again, the light fades, and the guards turn back around.
