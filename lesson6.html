CHAPTER VI by candlelight

The Crossroads

Past the well, the path forks in two — the first time this house has ever let you choose your own way. Both doors lead somewhere worth going. Only one opens tonight.

Read the carving above each door, and step through the one that calls to you. You can always return here later and take the other path another night.

The Spiral Stair

Behind this door, a staircase winds down and seems to repeat itself — the same step, again and again, until you tell it to stop. Learn to make the house do something more than once without writing it out by hand each time.

Descend the stair → the right door
The Second Question

Behind this door, a voice asks you one question — and if your answer doesn't satisfy it, asks another, and maybe another after that. Learn to give the house more than one way for a fork to turn out.
