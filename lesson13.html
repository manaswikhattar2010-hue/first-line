inside Autumn's Grove

You duck beneath the low branches and the grove opens up around you, warm and humming with quiet conversation. Before Autumn can even introduce you, someone barrels past her, grinning wide.

"You're the one who put my sister's fire out? Finally, someone interesting shows up." She sticks out a hand, practically vibrating. "I'm Ember. I have about a hundred questions, but I'll start with one."

Ember doesn't wait for spells to be written out fully beforehand — she likes to ask, and hear the answer right away. In the spellbook's tongue, this is input(): a spell that pauses, asks a question, and waits for whatever you type back before continuing.
